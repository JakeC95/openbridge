Renderer marks
==============

The Arnold name and mark are trademarks of Autodesk, Inc. The mark is used only
to identify the renderer in the OpenBridge UI.

- arnold.svg: the Autodesk Arnold symbol isolated from the official product
  lockup, retaining its transparent canvas. The source artwork is on Wikimedia
  Commons at https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Autodesk_Arnold_Logo.svg;
  that file identifies Autodesk's Arnold product page as its source:
  https://www.autodesk.com/products/arnold/overview.
