Renderer marks
==============

The Arnold name and mark are trademarks of Autodesk, Inc. The mark is used only
to identify the renderer in the OpenBridge UI.

- arnold.svg: the Autodesk Arnold symbol isolated from the official product
  lockup, retaining its transparent canvas. The source artwork is on Wikimedia
  Commons at https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Autodesk_Arnold_Logo.svg;
  that file identifies Autodesk's Arnold product page as its source:
  https://www.autodesk.com/products/arnold/overview.

- vray.svg: unmodified V-Ray color product icon from the Chaos V-Ray page
  https://www.chaos.com/vray. Artwork:
  https://static.chaos.com/images/assets/000/025/506/original/workflow-widget-v-ray-icon-color-32.svg
- mental-ray.png: NVIDIA's original compact mental ray product icon, not the
  NVIDIA corporate logo. See https://www.nvidia.com/es-la/nvem4/nvidia-mental-ray/
  and https://la.nvidia.com/content/company-info/images/nvidia-mentalray-icon.png.
- mantra.svg: SideFX's original Mantra render-node icon, not a standalone
  marketing logo. Removed an editor-only export path; artwork unchanged.
  See https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/nodes/out/ifd.html
  and https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/icons/ROP/mantra.svg.

The additional artwork and associated marks belong to their respective
owners. They identify renderer provenance and do not imply endorsement.

The Autodesk Maya, Houdini, and Nuke symbols are single-color Simple Icons
marks. Simple Icons is CC0-licensed; the marks remain trademarks of their
respective owners and are used only to identify software in the OpenBridge UI.
